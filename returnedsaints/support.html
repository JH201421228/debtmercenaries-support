FAQ

How does saving work?
The game saves automatically to your device when you return to the hub and when you change settings. There is no cloud save, so deleting the game deletes your progress.

Do I need the internet?
No. The game is fully offline.

How do I get a refund?
Through the store you bought it from (App Store: reportaproblem.apple.com / Google Play: order history).
